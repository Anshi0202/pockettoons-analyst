## Definition and scope

- The database covers **April 1–September 30, 2026**. I compared Q2 (**Apr–Jun**) with Q3 (**Jul–Sep**), the two complete quarters available.
- MAU is distinct users with at least one recorded session in a calendar month; quarterly MAU is the average of the three monthly counts. On that definition, MAU averaged **4,519** in Q2 and **5,308** in Q3. The stated **20%** increase is not reproduced by this data.
- Revenue is **successful transaction amounts in `amount_usd`, including negative refunds and excluding failed attempts**. This is a transaction-receipts measure, not necessarily accounting-recognized subscription revenue.
- As an engagement cross-check, distinct monthly viewers averaged **4,519** in Q2 and **5,031** in Q3 (**+11.3%**). The Q3 MAU lift is therefore not just a session-logging change, though not all session-active users watched content.
